## FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)

### Q1: Do you provide custom website design or use templates?
**A:** I provide both options! For unique branding, I create 100% custom designs tailored to your business. For budget-conscious clients, I can customize premium templates to match your brand. Custom designs are included in Standard and Premium packages, while the Basic package uses professionally customized templates.

### Q2: Will my website be mobile-friendly and responsive?
**A:** Absolutely! Every website I create is 100% mobile-responsive and tested across all devices (smartphones, tablets, laptops, desktops). I use mobile-first development approach ensuring your site looks perfect on screens of all sizes. Google prioritizes mobile-friendly sites, so this is standard in all packages.

### Q3: Do you provide hosting and domain?
**A:** I don't sell hosting or domains directly, but I'll guide you through the best options for your needs and budget. I recommend reliable providers like Namecheap, Bluehost, or AWS. I'll handle the complete setup and deployment to your hosting at no extra charge. If you don't have hosting yet, message me for recommendations.

### Q4: Can you redesign my existing website?
**A:** Yes! I specialize in website redesigns and modernization. I'll analyze your current site, maintain your SEO rankings, and create a fresh, modern design that converts better. I can work with any platform - WordPress, Shopify, custom-coded sites, etc. Share your current website URL for a detailed quote.

### Q5: How many revisions do I get?
**A:** Basic package includes 2 revisions, Standard includes 5, and Premium includes unlimited revisions. A revision means reasonable changes to design, content, or functionality we've discussed. Major scope changes may require additional fees. I always ensure you're 100% satisfied with the final result.

### Q6: Do you provide content writing services?
**A:** I include basic content formatting and upload in Standard and Premium packages. For professional copywriting, I can recommend talented writers or include it as an add-on service. I do provide SEO-optimized meta descriptions, titles, and basic content structure in all packages.

### Q7: Will I be able to update the website myself?
**A:** Yes! For WordPress and CMS-based sites, you'll get an easy-to-use admin panel. I provide video tutorials and documentation showing exactly how to update content, add blog posts, and manage your site. For custom-coded sites, I can integrate a content management system based on your needs.

### Q8: Do you offer e-commerce/online store development?
**A:** Absolutely! I build complete e-commerce solutions using WooCommerce, Shopify, or custom systems. This includes product catalogs, shopping carts, secure payment gateways (PayPal, Stripe, etc.), inventory management, order tracking, and customer accounts. E-commerce features are available in Standard and Premium packages.

### Q9: What about SEO and Google ranking?
**A:** All websites include basic on-page SEO: meta tags, schema markup, XML sitemap, robots.txt, fast loading speed, and mobile optimization. Premium package includes advanced SEO with keyword research, competitor analysis, and Google Search Console setup. This gives your site the best foundation for ranking.

### Q10: Do you provide support after delivery?
**A:** Yes! Basic package includes 7 days of bug fixes, Standard includes 14 days, and Premium includes 30 days of support. This covers any bugs or issues with features we built. For ongoing maintenance, updates, or new features, I offer affordable monthly maintenance packages. I'm always available for future work!

### Q11: Can you integrate third-party services and APIs?
**A:** Yes! I can integrate various services like payment gateways (Stripe, PayPal), email marketing (Mailchimp, SendGrid), CRM systems (HubSpot, Salesforce), booking systems (Calendly), social media feeds, Google Maps, chat widgets, and custom APIs. Complex integrations may require the Premium package.

### Q12: How long will it take to complete my website?
**A:** Delivery times: Basic (3 days), Standard (5-7 days), Premium (10-14 days). These are maximum timeframes - I often deliver earlier! Urgent projects can be expedited with the extra-fast delivery add-on. Timeline starts after I receive all required content and materials from you.

### Q13: What information do you need to start?
**A:** I'll need: 1) Your logo/branding materials, 2) Content (text, images) or content references, 3) Examples of websites you like, 4) Your color preferences, 5) List of pages needed, 6) Any specific features required. Don't have everything ready? No problem! I can help source stock images and guide content creation.

### Q14: Do you sign NDAs or confidentiality agreements?
**A:** Absolutely! I respect client confidentiality and happy to sign NDAs. Your business information, ideas, and data are completely safe. I never reuse client's unique features or share project details without permission. Professional ethics and client trust are my top priorities.
